## Summary of version 1

- **Vision and mission** (new) above the pillars: one vision sentence, a mission under 150 words.
- **Pillars** (3 to 5, about a 3-year horizon): a theme plus one dated, measurable long-term objective plus its strategies. At least one pillar
  faces the market and the customer.
- **Annual goals:** the year-one slice of each pillar's objective, quantified and consistent up and across.
- **Policies** (new): standing rules for recurring decisions (preferences, standards, the default reviewer). Links to DGS-271.
- **Quarterly OKRs:** 2 to 3 objectives, at most 4 key results each, scored as a percentage; one number, date, owner and baseline per key result;
  drafted by the maestros inside Jack's guardrails; each names its pillar.
- **Initiatives and epics:** a short business case, radical or incremental tag, staged token funding, one accountable maestro.
- **Tickets** carry the chain pillar → annual goal → OKR → epic.
- **Weekly scorecard:** 10 to 15 fast measures, balanced, actual vs expected, bad news mandatory, the verifier never owns the number.
- **Cadence:** weekly check-in, quarterly plan, review and retrospective (with a kill list), yearly review of the bases and scenarios.
- **Pillar #1, Token Economics:** an internal cost-leadership capability. Model access is a threshold, not an advantage; the first lever is
  the maestro and controller overhead (about 89% of DGS-214's cache reads).
- **Warnings from the sources:** six layers is heavier than any real example; strategy also emerges from below; over-formality; one owner per
  ticket; agents game what is measured.
