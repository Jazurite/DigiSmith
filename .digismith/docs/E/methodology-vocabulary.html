Methodology vocabulary

Clan E: Methodology Decided by Jack, 2026-10-03 Reference doc

The umbrella word

Methodology is the umbrella. It means the way DigiSmith works. Clan E: Methodology holds it. Every term below is one size of methodology.

The old roadmap in MEMORY.md already had a map item G, "Methodology". That item holds the house rules: standards injection (G.1), toolchain defaults (G.2) and doc conventions (G.3). Clan E keeps the same order, and adds the workflows as E.4.

The four purple rows are the values of the ClickUp field Kind (next section).

The Kind field

A drop-down custom field in ClickUp. It says what size of method a ticket defines.

  • Process: a chain of steps for one goal.
  • Protocol: the handshake between two roles. Flux (DGS-154) is the first one.
  • Procedure: the steps for one activity inside a protocol or process.
  • Primitive: one node, a skill.

The other terms are not values of Kind:

  • A workflow is the lineage itself, so it needs no tag.
  • A policy is a standard. It lives in E.1: Standards.
  • A runbook is a document that bundles procedures. It is not a ticket kind.
  • A checklist has no routing. It is a part of a procedure.
Status on 2026-10-03: the field is not created yet. The CLI and the ClickUp client cannot create fields. The field is created in ClickUp, in the E: Methodology folder.

Decisions

  • 2026-10-03: Primitives are single nodes. Workflows connect them. A workflow is not a primitive, so it gets its own lineage.
  • 2026-10-03: The lineage takes the name of the parent: Workflows.
  • 2026-10-03: The umbrella is one word, like the other clan names. "Ways of working" was rejected. "Methodology" is the word, and it matches map item G.
  • 2026-10-03: Workflows became a clan of their own: E: Methodology. E is the next free ClickUp letter. The empty list A.2: Protocols in clan A is left behind.
  • 2026-10-03: E.3 is Conventions, to match the map's G.3. Workflows is E.4.
