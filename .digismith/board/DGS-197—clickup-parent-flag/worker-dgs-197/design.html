DGS-197: --parent for dg clickup

Test result

  • Create a subtask in another list than its parent: 400 "Parent not child of list".
  • Create a subtask in the parent's list: 200.
  • Update a task in another list with parent: 200, and ClickUp moves the task into the parent's list.

Design

  • ClickUpTaskWriteBody.parent?: string | null.
  • buildTaskWriteBody: --parent X sends parent: "X"; --parent "" sends parent: null.
  • create-task and update-task get the option. No other change.
  • Regroup consequence: update-task --parent alone is enough; no separate move-task needed. The move may remap statuses.
