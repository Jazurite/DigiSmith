DGS-325 own domain for Nexus — Implementation Report

Ticket: DGS-325Date: 2026-10-13

Summary

pazurite.com (Nexus, /api/v1/...) and opencode.pazurite.com (OpenCode at /) become DNS-only Cloudflare records that point to the Workbox's tailnet address 100.108.1.19, so only Jack's devices with Tailscale on reach them. Tailscale Serve keeps port 443 and forwards the raw TCP stream to Caddy on 127.0.0.1:8443. Caddy ends TLS, gets its certificates by DNS challenge, Caddy listens on loopback only. The workbox.tail730dcf.ts.net name is dropped (Jack's decision): Caddy's Tailscale certificate getter cannot reach the local tailscaled, that URL stops working at the cut-over (J7), and Jack re-pairs the OpenCode Desktop app with https://opencode.pazurite.com. The rollback brings it back. The design and plan are approved by Jack. Build tasks 1-3 are done and on the branch (not merged); the depot holds the pinned Caddy, the Caddyfile, caddy-run and the rendered plist, and no service is loaded.

Findings that shaped the design

  • A non-root process on macOS 26.7.1 cannot bind port 443 on a specific address; only 0.0.0.0 works, and that includes the company LAN. Hence the raw TCP forward.
  • Caddy v2.11.7 with caddy-dns/cloudflare v0.2.4 comes pre-built from Caddy's download service, and the hash is reproducible.
  • A throwaway Caddy on 127.0.0.1:18443 streams SSE, passes a websocket upgrade, and refuses connections on the LAN and tailnet addresses.
  • Tailscale Serve did not answer on the IPv6 address from the Mac, so the Shopify AAAA is removed instead of repointed.

Known gaps

  • Done: Caddyfile, caddy-run.sh, plist template, install.sh, test-config.mjs and test-stream.mjs, all passing; install.sh ran for real.
  • Open: the authenticated SSE and websocket proof on the live OpenCode server needs its password, which no agent reads. Through a throwaway Caddy, /, /api/event (401) and a websocket upgrade (401) match the direct answers.
  • The ts.net certificate through Caddy is untested until the loopback proof; Desktop-app pairing can only be tested at the cut-over.

Staging certificate

scripts/caddy/staging-cert.sh obtained Let's Encrypt staging certificates for pazurite.com and opencode.pazurite.com by DNS challenge through Cloudflare, on loopback only with skip_install_trust and a temp data dir. The token was never printed. No service was loaded.
