Summary

The Nexus scan job could not prompt the conductor, because herdr-ws refuses to run without HERDR_WS and the job has none. The conductor workspace now lives in conductors.json and promptConductor sets HERDR_WS from it. A missing or unreadable setting stops the scan with an attention item instead of a silent claim-failed. Followed the full Superpowers process: spec and plan, digismith:subagent-driven-development (3 tasks, each dispatched to a fresh implementer subagent and independently reviewed) → a final whole-branch review, plus 1 fix round before merge.

Reference documents: the design spec and the implementation plan.

What Was Delivered

Task 1

Sets HERDR_WS from conductors.json

promptConductor reads the validated workspace from conductors.json and passes it to herdr-ws as the env, always over the job's own value; realExec takes an env argument.

Task 2

Scan reports a bad conductor config

The scan stops before any claim, writes an attention item and a conductor-config event, shows the problem in the dry run, and resolves the item on a clean read.

Task 3

Whole check

Full CLI suite, strip-types smoke and typecheck; no commit.
