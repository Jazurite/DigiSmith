What and why

digismith:brainstorming is an umbrella skill ("use before any creative work"). DGS-671 splits the work by role: the new digismith:shaping owns the product level (what, why, appetite, scope), and brainstorming becomes architecture and solution design for a ticket in refinement (DGS-488). This ticket makes that change in brainstorming and takes it out of the vendored list. The shaping skill itself is DGS-673.

Input

bootstrap, adopt and init hand a ticket to brainstorming as its seed. For Jira work the seed is ticket.md from jira-intake and has no ## Pitch. A hard rule "no pitch, stop" would break that route. So brainstorming accepts either shaped input:

  • a ClickUp ticket with a ## Pitch in its description, or
  • a ticket.md from jira-intake (title, description, acceptance criteria).

If the input has no problem and no scope, brainstorming stops. A ClickUp ticket goes to digismith:shaping, a Jira ticket goes to jira-intake Door 2. Brainstorming never asks again for purpose, scope, appetite or success criteria.

Edits to skills/brainstorming/SKILL.md

PartChange
Description"Architecture and solution design for a ticket in refinement, from a shaped input." The "MUST use before any creative work" trigger is dropped. The provenance tag becomes "DigiSmith-owned, forked from Superpowers' brainstorming".
Intro and checklistRewritten for the new role. A new "Input" step comes first.
"Understanding the idea"Questions on purpose, scope and success criteria, and the "too many subsystems, decompose" paragraph are removed. The interview rules (one question at a time, multiple choice) stay, for technical questions only. "Design for isolation and clarity" stays: it is technical decomposition.
New step: find the elementsA short, narrow element list. For flows, a breadboard in words: places (a command, a status, a file), affordances (what can be done there), connections (what leads where). A fat marker sketch only when layout is the core problem. Rough: boundaries and rules, not a spec.
New step: address rabbit holesStart from the pitch's flagged Risks. Walk the use case in slow motion, start to end. Question each part that looks solved: does it need new technical work, does it assume how parts fit, does it assume a design answer exists? Fix each hole with one of three moves: patch (dictate a simpler answer, written into the spec), declare out of bounds, or cut back. A hole with no answer goes to Jack.
Back-edgeIf the design cannot fit the appetite after cutting, stop and tell Jack the ticket goes back to shaping. No Steward event exists for that move yet, so it is done by hand.
Unchanged2-3 approaches, presenting the design, the design.html shell and path rules, the toolchain check, the visual companion, spec self-review, the user review gate, step-end, the hand-off to writing-plans.

Un-vendor

Remove "brainstorming" from VENDORED_SKILLS in scripts/check_vendored_skills.ts, then run node scripts/check_vendored_skills.ts and confirm it passes for the remaining skills. Brainstorming is then a DigiSmith-owned skill and the upstream drift check no longer covers it.

Merge order and risks

  • Merge order: DGS-674 (Steward event), DGS-673 (shaping skill), then this ticket. The work can be built earlier. Until it merges, brainstorming and the shaping skill overlap, which is harmless. This avoids a gap where a refinement ticket has no pitch and nowhere to go.
  • Not touched: init, bootstrap and adopt keep calling brainstorming unchanged. using-superpowers is a separate vendored skill and stays.
  • Risk: a ClickUp ticket already in refinement without a ## Pitch is stopped once this merges. The Maestro shapes it first (manually until the skill exists).
