What and why

DGS-671 makes concept mean "shaped". The shaping skill (DGS-673) ends by firing a Steward event that moves the ticket from backlog to concept, and it marks its token step as shaping. This ticket adds both. Technical decisions below were taken by the Maestro (Jack's rule: technical is the Maestro's call).

The shaped event

  • shaped replaces concept-started in EVENTS (packages/cli/src/steward/types.ts) and in the entry table (rules.ts): from backlog, into concept.
  • No quote and not a gate: it is not in GATE_EVENTS. The two gates stay as they are.
  • From concept it is a no-op (the existing viaEntry rule: status already equals the target). This covers tickets already in concept that get shaped later: the skill fires shaped, nothing moves, the call succeeds.
  • concept-started is removed with no alias. Nothing in skills or scripts fires it (checked by search). The CLI choices come from EVENTS, so dg steward event concept-started is then refused by the parser.
  • discovery-started and refinement-started are unchanged (still from concept).

The shaping step

Add "shaping" to STEPS in packages/cli/src/tokens/types.ts, before "brainstorming". Step, the registry check and the --step validation all derive from that array, so nothing else changes. Existing registry lines stay valid.
