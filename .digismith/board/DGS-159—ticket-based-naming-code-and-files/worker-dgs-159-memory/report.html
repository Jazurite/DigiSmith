1. Summary

DGS-159 Part 5 restructured MEMORY.md at the repo root so it reflects ticket-based naming (DGS-158): the ticket is the unit of work, not clan letters or lineage numbers. Everything already recorded in the old clan-letter map, build order, and open questions stayed word for word, moved into one labelled frozen section. New sections explain where work is tracked now (ClickUp), and one line in skills/handoff/SKILL.md was updated to match. Design and plan were approved at three checkpoints with the maestro and Jack before any edit to MEMORY.md was made.

2. What moved

The Map (26 rows, A–Y, plus the shared JIRA write-back primitive), Build Order (6 tiers, including its explanatory paragraphs), and the original Open Questions (3 items) moved verbatim — byte for byte, nothing reworded, nothing reordered internally — into one new section, "Frozen: the clan-letter map (through DGS-157)", at the end of the file. Their own headings were demoted from ## to ### (the only textual change touching them at all — a structural wrapper, not recorded content). Philosophy, Structure, What Superpowers Already Covers, Relationship to 2. Career/, and Conventions were not edited (Conventions got one new bullet appended; nothing existing in it changed). The two inline map-letter references inside Relationship to 2. Career/ (map item C, Tier 6) and inside Conventions (map items O, P) were deliberately left alone — they remain resolvable via the frozen section further down the same file.
