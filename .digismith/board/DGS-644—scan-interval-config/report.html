Summary

The Nexus job autonomous-scan runs every 30 minutes instead of every 5. The interval is the setting nightwatch.scan_every_minutes in .digismith/config.yml (whole number 5 to 720, default and fallback 30). The scheduler reads it at every tick, so a change needs no API restart. A bad or missing value falls back to 30 and the watchdog opens a bad-scan-interval-config item; the scan never stops. The other three 5-minute jobs are unchanged.

Reference documents: the spec and plan (one page). Final review: clean (claude-opus-5-5), two Minor findings, both fixed. Review findings: findings.md. Report text: report.md.
