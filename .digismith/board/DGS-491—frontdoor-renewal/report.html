DGS-491 Frontdoor renewal — Implementation Report

Ticket: DGS-491Date: 2026-10-16

Summary

The Frontdoor client now renews its own 48-hour session with a stored refresh cookie, and dg clickup frontdoor refresh does it by hand. No Proxyman capture is needed every two days. The whole suite passes (2322 tests).

What changed

  • The Frontdoor token comes from POST id.app.clickup.com/data/v3/workspaces/<team>/authentication/access_tokens (empty body, cookies cu_refresh and cu_session_meta), not from /auth/v1/refresh_token as the ticket said: that call returns a token without the workspace_id claim. The cookie does not rotate and lasts about a year.
  • clickup-client: renewSession, FileSessionStore (atomic write, mode 600, other lines kept; lock file, stale after 60 s). FrontdoorClient renews before a call when the token expires in under 10 minutes or is expired, and once on a 401/403 with one retry. Without a stored cookie nothing changes.
  • CLI: frontdoor refresh; import-auth stores the cookie and prints renewal: yes/no; README and --help.
  • Only the two-cookie call is built; no User-Agent or CSRF fallback.

Tests

Written first, with a fake store, a fake transport and temp dirs. Fixture secrets are asserted absent from stdout, stderr and errors. Full suite: 155 files, 2322 tests.

Whole-branch review

Claude subagent: no leak, nothing critical. Four fixes with tests: a 15 s timeout on the renewal call, a lock released only by its owner, the secret temp file removed when the rename fails, and a failed pre-call renewal no longer blocks a call while the token has time left. Parked: one renewal per 403, duplicate keys in .env, quote round trip, a narrow stale-lock race.
